# Fengsheng support

Email support@metag-ai.com for sign-in, production or delivery help. Include your job ID if a job was created. Keep passwords, access tokens and private download links out of support messages.

To start from a film made with METAG, export the cut you want to use. In the Fengsheng upload guide, sign in with the same account you use for your chat connection. Open the media link from your saved brief and choose the clip there. Return to the same conversation after the upload. The general upload page also offers a reference you can paste into chat. You choose when the sound brief goes into production. [Upload guide](https://metag-ai.com/guide/#chat-upload)

If a queue request times out, ask about the same conversation before starting another one. On completion, request current download links for that job. You can contact support for a deletion request. [Privacy policy](https://metag-ai.com/privacy.html)

### Try it in ChatGPT

In ChatGPT on the web, open Plugins, select the plus button and choose **Add custom MCP server**. Enter the name Fengsheng and the server URL `https://metag-ai.com/fengsheng/mcp`. Select OAuth and create the plugin. Install it in your workspace and authorize the Fengsheng account used for your uploads. Workspace permissions apply. Start with “Which sound styles are available?” [OpenAI connection guide](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/connect-chatgpt)

If a brief is waiting for a clip, ask Fengsheng to reopen the saved draft and show its media link. On that page, confirm the shared ChatGPT file when available, or choose the original on your device. Once the page confirms the attachment, return to chat and review the updated brief. If your client still lists the previous tools, refresh the connection.

For a repeatable example, download the four-second clapper cut and upload it in the guide. Ask to preserve the two impacts, select the unscored style, and review the brief before starting. After you approve it, ask for the job status and the delivered files. The walkthrough shows the source and the resulting mix from a production run with an internal test account.
